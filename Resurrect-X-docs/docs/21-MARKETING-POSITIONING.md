# 21 — Marketing Positioning

## Category

**Private, on-premise AI workspace for legal practices.**

Avoid positioning it as an “AI lawyer”. The customer buys controlled access to their own knowledge and workflows, not automated legal accountability.

## Core value proposition

> Give your firm the usefulness of an AI assistant while keeping production knowledge, permissions and action controls inside the firm's own boundary.

## Message pillars

### 1. Your knowledge becomes queryable

Search matters, precedents and internal records through a single conversational surface.

### 2. The source stays visible

Professional work needs verification. Resurrect X keeps supporting passages beside the answer.

### 3. Acting is different from answering

Read-only and read/write are explicit modes. The model can suggest; policy decides.

### 4. Production can stay local

Local inference, local indexes and firm-controlled storage are the intended production baseline.

### 5. Connections are exceptions, not assumptions

Web/provider/integration routes should be visible and individually governed.

## Ideal homepage copy skeleton

### Hero

**Private AI for the work your firm cannot send away.**

Search files, check sources, draft from your precedents, and run approved actions on infrastructure your firm controls.

CTA: `See the workspace` / `Book a controlled demo`

### Section — It knows the matter, not the whole firm

Matter-level scope and access controls ensure the assistant works inside the same boundaries as the user.

### Section — Read. Or act.

**Read only** for search, summarisation and cited drafting.  
**Read & write** for approved, versioned output creation.

### Section — Source first

Show a contract answer with source viewer.

### Section — Local production

Illustrate browser → appliance → local model/index → NAS. External connection dashed and off by default.

### Section — Built for legal work

Cards:

- contract review;
- matter chronology;
- precedent search;
- correspondence draft;
- discovery summary;
- institutional knowledge.

### Section — Security without theatre

Explain concrete controls:

- server-side authorisation;
- matter-scoped retrieval;
- default-deny local egress;
- restricted tool set;
- versioned writes;
- audit trail.

### CTA

**Bring a redacted workflow. We’ll show you exactly what the system reads, where it runs, and what it is allowed to do.**

## Buyer objection responses

### “Why not just use a public AI tool?”

Public tools can be excellent for low-risk/general work. Resurrect X is intended for workflows where the location of processing, source access, audit and action permissions matter.

### “Are local models smart enough?”

The product should be benchmarked against the firm's actual jobs. Local models are especially useful when the answer depends on private matter/precedent context; frontier connected models can remain an optional exception for approved low-risk work if a firm chooses.

### “Can it modify our files?”

Not in read-only mode. In read/write mode, early versions write only to controlled output locations, with versioning and policy checks.

### “Is it prompt-injection-proof?”

Do not make that claim. Say: the architecture assumes model/source text can be manipulated and prevents that text from granting additional authority.

## Brand voice

- Australian English.
- Short sentences.
- Specific architectural nouns.
- No hype superlatives unless measurable.
- No provider name as the product identity.
- No fear-mongering about competitors.
