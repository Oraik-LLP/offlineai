# 19 — Supplied Website References

Research date: 18 September 2026.

This document analyses the **observable product and presentation patterns** of the supplied websites. Public webpages do not reliably reveal the private backend/database stack, so backend claims are not guessed unless explicitly described by the site itself.

## 1. LocallyAI

URL: https://locallyai.com.au/

### What it publicly positions

- On-premise/private AI for Australian businesses.
- Browser “Harness” running on hardware the customer owns.
- Document search, meetings, research, tools, scheduled work and source citations.
- Local/offline base workflow with external connections treated as explicit exceptions.
- Multiple hardware paths including Mac, AMD/Windows/Linux and NVIDIA DGX Spark.

### Useful product lessons for Resurrect X

- Explain the processing boundary plainly.
- Show actual office jobs, not only technical features.
- Keep sources close to answers.
- Treat integrations/remote support/web research as explicit exceptions.
- Sell the working system, not merely a model.

### UI lesson

Strong product structure and trust explanation; Resurrect X should be more law-specific and visually more restrained/premium.

## 2. LocalPod

URL: https://localpod.ai/

### What it publicly positions

- On-premise “AI in a box”.
- Digital worker that connects to business systems.
- Strong offline/privacy messaging.
- Calls, calendar/CRM/knowledge integrations.

### Useful product lesson

The “one local digital worker that grows with permitted integrations” concept is useful for later agentic phases.

### Do not copy

Project direction explicitly says this is **not** the desired website visual style.

---

## 3. Apothy

URL: https://www.apothyai.com/

### What it publicly positions

- Private/sovereign AI for institutions that cannot expose data.
- Cloud or on-premise deployment.
- Assistant embedded into firm operations.
- Role-based/auditable, source-grounded professional workflows.
- Premium institutional tone.

### UI/brand lessons

- High-quality editorial typography.
- Controlled use of product screenshots.
- Short, confident sections.
- “Inside the boundary” visual language.
- Strong fit for legal/professional-services marketing inspiration.

### Resurrect X differentiation

Make the permission/mode architecture more explicit and show read-only/read-write containment as a signature feature.

---

## 4. InThePond

URL: https://inthepond.com.au/

### What it publicly positions

- AI-native agency.
- Enterprise AI, local assistants, automation and technical builds.
- Sovereign/local AI deployments.
- Engineering-heavy case-study/editorial presentation.

### UI lessons

- Excellent restraint.
- Strong type and whitespace.
- Small number of sharp sections.
- Case-study credibility instead of endless feature cards.

Use this for the **feel** of the marketing site more than the product architecture.

---

## 5. Ermos

URL: https://www.ermos.com.au/

### What it publicly positions

- On-premise sovereign AI hardware/services for regulated professional firms.
- Explicit law-firm use cases such as contract review, discovery summaries, drafting and institutional knowledge.
- Strong focus on keeping client data inside the organisation.
- Practical objection handling and legal-market content.

### Product/marketing lessons

- Speak directly to law-firm jobs.
- Address privilege/confidentiality concerns without drowning the buyer in engineering details.
- Put the appliance and processing location at the centre of the story.
- Explain where local models are strongest: firm-specific knowledge/workflows rather than every possible general-knowledge task.

### Important caution

Resurrect X should not adopt claims like “physically unable to leave” unless the deployed network controls and optional connections truly make that accurate for the specific configuration.

---

## 6. Recommended synthesis for Resurrect X

### Marketing visual direction

**Apothy + InThePond + Ermos**

- premium editorial design;
- clear law-firm specificity;
- fewer, stronger sections;
- real product UI screenshots;
- no generic AI visual clichés.

### Product/IA direction

**LocallyAI + Resurrect X's stricter policy model**

- browser workspace;
- sources beside answer;
- matter/file organisation;
- local appliance;
- explicit connected exceptions;
- two-mode execution model;
- classification-enforced permissions.

### Product positioning line to explore

> **Private AI that works inside the boundaries your firm already trusts.**

Alternative:

> **Search the file. Check the source. Let the AI act only when you allow it.**

## 7. Public stack-analysis limitation

From ordinary public pages, exact private backend technologies, databases and internal deployment architecture are not reliably knowable. Do not create competitor “backend” claims from guesswork. For Resurrect X, use these references for observable UX, copy structure, product packaging and public architecture statements only.
